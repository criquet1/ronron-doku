🐾 Ronron-doku 🐾

Logic game with cats · grids from 5 × 5 up to 15 × 15

Rules

Place one cat in every row, every column and every colour zone. Two cats must never touch, even diagonally.

Click a cell: ✖ (excluded), then the cat, then empty.

The ✖ are placed automatically around each cat.

With a mouse, you can also drag across several cells to place ✖.

Settings

Click a colour to change it. One colour per zone of the current grid.

About Ronron-doku

Ronron-doku is a free logic game that you play right in your browser, on a computer or a phone, with no sign-up and no ads.

The goal: place one cat in every row, every column and every colour zone, so that no two cats touch, even diagonally. Every grid has exactly one solution.

  • Grids from 5 × 5 up to 15 × 15.
  • The ✖ are placed automatically around the cats, and you can also make the excluded cells disappear.
  • A “Hint” button that explains the reasoning step by step.
  • An “Undo” button to take back as many moves as you like.
  • A game code: with the same code, you get the same grid on any device.
  • Several colour palettes, and you can pick the colour of each zone.
  • Your game in progress is saved automatically.
